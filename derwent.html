Derwent Crossing

Cross the Derwent at Hobart on a pump foil: no motor, no sail. Lie down and paddle, pop up when you're fast enough, then pump to stay flying. Catch wind chop and the ferry's wake to rest your legs. Fall off and you're back on your belly, paddling.
