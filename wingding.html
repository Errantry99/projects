Wing Ding

Light wind, big foil. Pump the wing and the board to get up on foil, then keep it flying: not so high the foil breaches, not so low you touch down, and don't let it roll.

WS or ↑↓Lean forward / back (nose down / up)
AD or ←→Shift weight left / right (roll balance)
SpacePump the board (one stroke per press)
K or ShiftDing the wing (one wing pump per press)
P / Esc · RPause · restart

Pumps work best in a steady rhythm: wing about every half second, board a little slower. Mashing gives weak strokes and burns stamina. Lean back to pop up, then lean forward as speed builds.
